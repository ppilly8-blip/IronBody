# IronBody – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

**In short:** IronBody does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. What you do in the app stays on your own device.

The same text is published as a web page: <https://ppilly8-blip.github.io/IronBody/privacy.html>

## Who is behind IronBody

IronBody is a free workout app published by Preetam Pilly. Its source code is public at <https://github.com/ppilly8-blip/IronBody>, so anyone can check what the app does.

## What the app saves on your device

To work properly, IronBody keeps a little information in the app's own storage on your phone or browser:

- **Your progress:** which sets you finished and on which day (for your streak, totals and badges).
- **Your settings:** AUTO or MANUAL mode, rep pace, and whether sound cues are on.

This information never leaves your device. The developer cannot see it, and the app does not upload it anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- It does not show ads and does not use advertising IDs.
- It does not contain analytics, crash reporting, trackers or other third-party code that collects data.
- It does not need an internet connection to work. After it is installed, every exercise, picture and timer is inside the app.

## Permissions on Android

The Android app needs no sensitive permissions. It uses vibration to buzz at the end of a set, and it never asks for access to your files, location, camera, microphone or contacts. Android also lists the standard "internet access" permission that web-based apps carry; IronBody does not use it to send your data anywhere.

## Deleting your data

- In the app: open **Progress** and choose **Reset progress**.
- Or clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or uninstall the app. Everything IronBody saved is then removed.

If your phone's own backup feature is switched on, Android may include the app's saved data in _your_ backup in your Google account. That is controlled by your phone settings; the developer has no access to it.

## Using the web version

If you open IronBody in a browser, the page is delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs (for example your IP address) when the page is loaded; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). IronBody itself does not add any tracking to the page.

## Children

IronBody is a general fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, there is no children's data to collect or remove.

## Health notice

IronBody offers general exercise ideas, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, injured or have a health condition, and stop if something hurts.
