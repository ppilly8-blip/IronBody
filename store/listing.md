## Store listing (English)

**App name** (23/30)
IronBody: Home Workouts

**Short description** (80/80)
No-equipment home workouts with hands-free timers. Bodyweight, HIIT, core, yoga.

**Full description** (1585/4000)

IronBody turns your phone into a workout coach that needs no equipment, no account and no internet.

Pick a routine, tap START and let the app run it for you: a GET READY countdown, a steady rep count for every set, a timer for every hold, a rest between sets, then the next exercise. Keep your eyes on your form, not on your phone.

WHAT YOU GET
• 26 ready-made routines: beginner, HIIT, strength, core, cardio, yoga, stretching and isometric holds
• 74 exercises with step-by-step pictures, the muscles they work and a form cue
• AUTO mode: counts your reps at a steady pace, switches sides for you and moves on by itself. Prefer your own speed? Switch to MANUAL with one tap
• Pace control: normal, slow or fast
• Hold timers with sound and vibration cues
• Rest timer between sets, with pause and skip
• Interval timer with your own work, rest and rounds
• Progress that only counts what you really did: workouts, sets, minutes, day streak and a weekly view
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking

FOR EVERY LEVEL
Start with short beginner sessions, build up with HIIT, strength and core circuits, then try the advanced isometric and bodyweight programs. A 5-minute desk stretch and gentle yoga are there for easy days.

PRIVACY FIRST
IronBody does not ask for an account and does not collect or share personal data. Your progress is saved on your device only. You can delete it any time from the Progress tab.

IMPORTANT
IronBody gives general exercise ideas, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before you start a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts.

**Release notes (first release)**
First release: 26 routines, 74 exercises with step-by-step pictures, hands-free AUTO mode with pace control, hold and interval timers, and progress tracking. Works offline.
